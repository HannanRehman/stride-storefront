# Pakistan streetwear name ideas

Researched October 14, 2026. Keep Stride as the temporary website identity until a name is chosen.

## Naming direction

The current [Rastah hoodie collection](https://pk.rastah.co/collections/core-ss-26-hoodies) and [Groovy storefront](https://groovypakistan.com/) show two useful local directions: a South Asian identity and a short expressive English identity. This is a naming inference from these examples, not a popularity ranking or proof of demand. The proposed names below are creative suggestions; trademark, business-name, domain and social-handle availability have not been checked.

| Name | Direction | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Raakh Studio** | Dark, restrained streetwear | Raakh means ash; suits charcoal hoodies, washed fabrics and quiet graphics. |
| **Gali Supply** | Local, casual streetwear | Gali means street/lane; memorable for tees, hoodies and everyday pieces. |
| **Dhund** | Atmospheric, minimal | Dhund means fog; short and visually strong on a wordmark or embroidered hoodie. |
| **Beyrang** | Monochrome basics | An interpretation of “without colour”; fits a black, white and grey collection. |
| **Shehr Dept.** | City-focused streetwear | Shehr means city; leaves space for collections inspired by different Pakistani cities. |
| **Raat Club** | Evening, youth-oriented | Raat means night; suits darker colours and graphic drops. |
| **Sukoon Supply** | Comfortable everyday clothing | Sukoon means calm/comfort; a useful direction for relaxed hoodies and lounge pieces. |
| **Slowform** | English, understated | A compact name for relaxed silhouettes and considered basics. |

Preferred shortlist: Raakh Studio for a premium restrained direction, Gali Supply for accessible streetwear, and Dhund for a minimal single-word identity. Check availability before printing labels or choosing a domain. These ideas do not authorize copying any existing brand's logo or content.
