# Original assets

All seven bitmap assets were created for this project using the **built-in imagegen tool**, without input/reference images. No image was downloaded from Nike or another retailer. They illustrate original demo concepts rather than real merchandise. Compressed WebP copies are saved in `public/images/`; originals remain in the generator's output folder. Only resizing/format compression was applied for website delivery.

| Saved asset | Use |

## Final generation prompts

### hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original sportswear storefront widescreen hero photograph, 16:9. A young adult female runner with dark hair wearing plain unbranded charcoal performance shirt and shorts, light gray generic running shoes, captured mid-stride on a coastal concrete path next to tall dry grass, pale blue sky, slate ocean, dramatic early morning side light. Full body runner occupies the RIGHT THIRD of the frame; the LEFT HALF contains darker slate coastal hillside and spacious landscape where white HTML text will be overlaid. Editorial athletic photography, real skin and fabric texture, quiet cinematic desaturated blue and taupe palette, natural film grain. No text, no letters, no logos, no swooshes, no recognizable branded shoe designs, no watermark. Original composition.

### court

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original sportswear campaign portrait photograph, 4:5. Athletic young adult male on a terracotta outdoor basketball court in the city, holding a basketball at his hip, plain black sleeveless top, long pale gray shorts, generic unbranded gray sneakers. Candid full body with much negative space around him, textured concrete architecture, late afternoon natural sunlight, subtle film grain, fashion editorial composition. No text, no logos, no swooshes, no branded patterns, no watermark. Original scene.

### pace-shoe

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: original online store footwear product photograph, square. One unique original running sneaker in chalk white with graphite gray mesh accents and subtly ribbed sculptural sole; geometric rectangular panel construction, no curved swoosh-like marks, no logos, no letters. Side three-quarter profile, toe pointing left, entire shoe visible with generous space around it, floating slightly above a seamless very light gray background with soft natural shadow. Premium studio photography, detailed textile knit and matte rubber, white-gray monochromatic palette, no text, no watermark.
